Friction Setup Checklist
do once
1
Delete shopping apps from your phone
Amazon, ASOS, Depop, whatever you use. Force yourself to use a browser — it adds 2 minutes of friction that kills most impulse buys.
2
Remove saved cards from every website
Amazon, Uber Eats, everywhere. Typing in a card number gives your brain time to pause. It's annoying — that's the point.
3
Unsubscribe from all store emails
Use unroll.me or go through your inbox manually. Sales emails are dopamine traps designed by professionals. Opt out.
4
Open a separate savings account at a different bank
SoFi, Marcus, Ally — all free, good rates. Set up an auto-transfer on payday. Never log in casually.
5
Set up a "fun money" debit card
Load it with $400 at the start of the month. When it's empty, fun spending stops. No shame, no tracking needed — the card does the work.
6
Install a website blocker for weak moments
Cold Turkey or Freedom — schedule blocks for evenings/weekends when impulse spending peaks. Block Amazon, ASOS, UberEats during those windows.

Click each step to check it off as you complete it.
